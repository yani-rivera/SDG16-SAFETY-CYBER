Every country on both scales · dashed lines split low, middle and high

Each measure is split into three groups of roughly equal size, low, middle and high, across the countries that have it. The dashed lines mark those splits. Ranks: 1 is the highest value, and tied values share a rank. Some scores come in half steps, so many countries share a value; in the scatter plot those dots are nudged by a small random amount so none hides another. Exact values are in the tooltips and the country card. Grey countries lack one or both values.
